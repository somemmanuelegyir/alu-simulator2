    Engineering Information

    Word size

    Operands and results are 4 bits wide (0–15, 0000–1111). Every result is masked with 0b1111, so overflow wraps around exactly as it would in a 4-bit register.

    Flags

    Carry = 1 when A + B exceeds 15.
    Borrow = 1 when A < B in a subtraction.
    Zero = 1 when the 4-bit result is 0000.
    Carry and Borrow are 0 for operations where they do not apply.

    Subtraction

    SUB is shown the way hardware performs it: A + (~B) + 1 in two's complement, keeping the low four bits. The carry-out of that addition is the inverse of Borrow.

    Scope of v1.0.0

    A functional model written in JavaScript. It is not a gate-level circuit, an HDL description, or an FPGA design. Those are planned for later versions.
